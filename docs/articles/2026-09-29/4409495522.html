India needs to boost domestic oil and gas exploration to enhance its energy security amid supply disruptions that drive energy prices higher, according to a senior official at state-owned exploration and production company Oil India Limited (OIL).

IOL财务总监Abhijit Majumder在当地媒体报道的印度能源行业活动中表示： “全球供应链和主要能源走廊的中断可能会对全球能源流动、价格和经济产生直接影响。”

The Strait of Hormuz crisis is one example of how geopolitical instability in one corner of the world could threaten energy security in all other regions, the executive said.

India’s government has an ambitious plan to boost exploration, following three consecutive years in which domestic crude oil production has declined.

This year, the federal government launched the so-called National Offshore Exploration Scheme, with $8.8 billion in government support through FY 2030–31. The scheme is India’s most ambitious offshore exploration mission to date.

作为该计划的一部分，政府将提供高达50 ％的深水勘探井钻探成本的财政支持。据政府称，这项支持将降低勘探风险，鼓励投资，并加速印度海上碳氢化合物潜力的开发。

India's future hydrocarbon potential lies largely in deepwater and ultra-deepwater basins such as Krishna-Godavari, Cauvery, Mahanadi, and the Andaman region. Exploration in these frontier areas requires advanced technology and significant investment, with a single deepwater exploratory well costing about $125–150 million.

联邦政府表示： “认识到这种勘探的高风险、高成本性质，政府正在采取风险分担方法，鼓励持续投资，加快勘探，并开采印度海上碳氢化合物资源。”

作为加强能源安全的一部分，印度还计划建设新的储存地点，因为世界第三大原油进口国希望提高其应对供应冲击的能力。
