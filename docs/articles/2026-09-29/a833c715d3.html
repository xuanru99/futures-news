(LEAD) (Asiad) S. Korea's Park Hye-jeong wins silver in women's weightlifting

日本名古屋9月29日电韩国的Park Hye-jeong周二在爱知-名古屋亚运会上在女子举重比赛中获得了最重的银牌。

In the women's +81-kilogram event final at Nagoya City Trade Industry Center in Nagoya, Japan, Park finished second with a total of 300 kilograms, with 133kg in the snatch and 167kg in the clean and jerk, behind Li Wenwen of China, who lifted a total of 310kg, with 140kg in the snatch and 170kg in the clean and jerk.

Bronze went to Ouisal Ikhlef of Qatar, who recorded a total of 273kg.

Park won the gold medal in the event at the previous Hangzhou Asian Games but failed to defend her Asiad title.

At the 2024 Paris Olympics, Park also won the silver medal behind Li.

抢劫继续进行Park和Li的交易创纪录的表演。

在她第一次尝试成功达到128公斤后，朴槿惠第二次举起了130公斤，创造了新的亚运会纪录。但李以132公斤的体重很快就超过了这个标准。然后朴以133公斤创造了另一项亚运会纪录，李以136公斤再次打破纪录。

在第三次尝试中，李以140公斤的新亚运会纪录完成了她的抢夺。

朴槿惠的133公斤是一项个人最佳成绩，也是一项新的全国纪录。

In the clean and jerk, the competition was less dramatic, with Li's gold and Park's silver effectively settled from the beginning.

Park在第二次尝试中举起了167公斤，创造了个人最佳成绩，并创造了她总重300公斤的全国纪录。然后，她尝试了173公斤的个人最好成绩，但失败了。

Li从166公斤开始，第二次尝试将体重提高到170公斤，总重达到310公斤，比Park提前10公斤。

Park说，她对自己的个人最佳成绩感到满意，这比她以前的最佳成绩高出2公斤，并帮助她达到了300公斤大关。

"Before I came here, I hoped to extend my record in the snatch, and I'm happy that I lifted 2kg more," she said. "I'll work harder to lift more than 300kg at the upcoming national competition and the world championships."
