Why Blocking U.S. Diesel Exports Could Make Fuel More Expensive

美国战略石油储备降至1982年以来的最低水平

俄罗斯不断增加的伤亡人数可能迫使……

分析师警告称，谈到可能禁止美国出口柴油以遏制油价飙升，最终可能会推高所有燃油价格，随着储油量的增加削减炼油厂的运行。此举可能导致美国的燃料供应过剩，而世界其他国家正在努力确保供应。

Retail diesel prices in the U.S. hit an all-time high of over $6.50 per gallon last week amid the global crunch that led to higher exports of the fuel, notably to Europe. In order to rein in prices for American drivers, legislators proposed a temporary ban on exports, and President Trump signaled he would get behind such a move.

Not all agree it would be the right move, however. Energy Secretary Chris Wright warned it would lead to some unintended consequences last week, saying that “The blunt tool of banning diesel exports definitely doesn't work. If you can't export the diesel that comes out of our refineries, you run out of places to store it, and you have to reduce US refining, which would put upward pressure on gasoline prices and jet fuel prices.”

Analysts concur. Wood Mackenzie said in a note last Thursday that a ban would quickly fill up storage space and force a sharp cut in refinery runs, which would “ultimately increase the volume and cost of gasoline imports, potentially shifting the cost burden from diesel to gasoline at the pump.”

The consultancy has estimated that a 90-day ban on diesel exports would result in the redirection of some 700,000 barrels of the fuel and gasoil to storage, and this would fill available storage space to the maximum in a little more than a month. As a result, refiners would be forced to cut their run rates by some 2 million barrels daily, meaning gasoline production would also suffer a reduction. Then again, energy companies could boost exports of crude by the same amount.

从表面上看，原油出口增加可能导致该能源商品（如果不是其衍生产品）的价格下降，但这种情况存在问题，该问题与美国以外的可用炼油能力有关。这种能力是有限的，而且都在中国。欧洲炼油厂特别短缺，因此从美国进口的燃料大幅增加。

“具有讽刺意味的是，美国柴油出口禁令可能会增加美国消费者的成本，” Wood Mackenzie的炼油、化工和石油市场高级副总裁Commodities Research表示。Alan Gelder解释说： “削减原油供应以管理供应过剩将使成本负担从柴油转向汽油，这意味着旨在缓解柴油泵的政策最终可能会推高汽油泵的价格。”他补充说： “中国目前是唯一一个拥有可弥补美国炼油厂吞吐量损失的备用炼油能力的国家。然而，中国很可能认为这样做不符合其利益。”

The talk of a diesel export ban has meanwhile already served to pressure U.S. crude oil prices. West Texas Intermediate is trading at a discount of $12 to Brent crude, even as both benchmarks book gains following President Trump’s rejection of Iran’s plan for a peace deal, which the latter presented during last week’s UN General Assembly session in New York.

通常情况下，这将提振对美国原油的需求。但是，上述一些关键市场的炼油厂产能有限，这干扰了事物的自然进程。最重要的是，中东战争导致的运费和保险成本上升，损害了石油需求与商品价格的关系。

由于油轮的可用性有限，运费飙升，由于中东的战争保费，保险费上涨。根据路透社的Ron Bousso上周引用的Signal Maritime数据，从墨西哥湾沿岸到亚洲的VLCC之旅现在花费约5000万美元。相比之下，在2月底美国-以色列与伊朗的战争开始之前，这一数字为1600万美元。即使在目前的挤压下，美国境外炼油产能紧张以及运费和保险成本上升的结合也扭转了石油需求的脚本。

Refiners in the United States produce 5.1 million barrels of diesel fuel daily. Exports run at 1.2 million barrels daily, according to JP Morgan data. Domestic consumption averages some 3.6 million barrels. Theoretically, there is enough diesel fuel both for the domestic market and for exports. However, the fact that both crude oil and diesel markets are global means price moves on that global market inevitably affect the domestic market as well. And it seems that a ban on diesel fuel exports, even for 90 days, would do more harm than good, notably to those whom it is supposed to help.
