S. Korea inks 640 bln-won deal to export 19 Chunmoo rocket launchers to Croatia

Yonhap · 2026-09-10 19:35 · 机器翻译，仅供参考

SEOUL, Sept. 10 (Yonhap) -- South Korea has signed a deal to export 18 Chunmoo multiple launch rocket systems (MLRS) to Croatia in an arms contract worth some 640 billion won (US$477 million), the state arms procurement agency said Thursday.

据国防采购计划管理局（ DAPA ）称，韩国国防公司韩华航空航天公司（ Hanwha Aerospace Co. ）周四（当地时间）与克罗地亚国防部签署了价值4.1亿欧元的协议。

这标志着韩国和克罗地亚签署的第一份重大防务合同。

克罗地亚已成为继波兰、爱沙尼亚和挪威之后收购Chunmoo系统的第四个欧洲国家。

Chunmoo MLRS主要用于应对朝鲜的远程火炮威胁，是一种韩国武器系统，能够使用各种类型的火箭和不同射程的导弹进行快速、精确的打击。

DAPA said it anticipates that exports of the Chunmoo system will serve as a stepping stone to expand long-term, strategic cooperation with local defense firms in Croatia, including on industrial cooperation and post-logistics support.

周四，国防部长Ahn Gyu-back和DAPA部长Lee Yong-cheol出席了由克罗地亚总理主持的签字仪式，并讨论了扩大双边防务合作的方法。
