（牵头） SK集团负责人参观蔚山在建AI数据中心

SEOUL, Sept. 10 (Yonhap) -- SK Group Chairman Chey Tae-won on Thursday visited an artificial intelligence (AI) data center under construction in the southeastern city of Ulsan and SK Innovation Co.'s nearby petrochemical complex, calling for greater synergy between the group's AI infrastructure and manufacturing operations.

Chey在2026年蔚山论坛前一天参观了位于该市国家工业园区的SK AI蔚山数据中心。据该集团称，他由SK Innovation、SK Telecom和SK Gas等主要SK子公司的首席执行官陪同。

SK Group Chairman Chey Tae-won (C) inspects the construction site of SK Group's artificial intelligence data center in the southeastern city of Ulsan on Sept. 10, 2026, in this photo provided by SK Innovation Co. (PHOTO NOT FOR SALE) (Yonhap)

The envisioned center is an AI-focused data center being jointly developed by SK Group and Amazon Web Services (AWS) at an estimated cost of 7 trillion won (US$5.2 billion).

During the visit, Chey described the envisioned data center as a key piece of South Korea's AI infrastructure, while pointing to SK Innovation's Ulsan Complex (CLX) as a major testing ground for applying AI to real-world manufacturing operations.

The Ulsan CLX, SK Innovation's flagship refining and petrochemical production base, has been pushing an AI-driven transformation of its operations, providing a potential link between the group's expanding AI computing infrastructure and its traditional manufacturing businesses.

Chey表示： “SK成为全栈AI提供商的愿景只有在SK AI数据中心蔚山（ AI基础设施的象征）与SK Innovation的蔚山CLX无缝连接时才能实现，其中AI应用于实际生产。”他呼吁SK集团子公司之间实现更大的协同效应。

施工始于去年8月的奠基仪式，数据中心计划于2027年下半年开始运营。

The facility is designed to support high-performance computing based on graphics processing units (GPUs) and incorporates hybrid cooling systems to provide greater performance and efficiency than conventional data centers.

该集团计划将蔚山数据中心作为吸引人工智能公司、创造就业机会和推动制造业创新的枢纽，作为工业城市的新增长引擎。
