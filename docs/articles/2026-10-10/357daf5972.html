McEwen以5500万$出售安大略省资产，助推黄金增长计划

Mining · 2026-10-09 23:45 · 机器翻译，仅供参考

McEwen (TSX: MUX) has agreed to sell two non-core gold properties in Ontario, Canada, to Discovery Mining (TSX: DSV) for $55 million in cash and stock as it shifts capital toward a production expansion targeting up to 300,000 gold-equivalent ounces annually by 2030.

The transaction includes Lexam VG Gold’s wholly owned Fuller property, its 60% interest in the Paymaster property and a parcel of surface rights owned by VG Holdings. Both properties are part of McEwen’s Fox Complex land holdings in the Timmins mining district.

Fuller占地约210公顷，而Paymaster占地179公顷。Discovery通过其子公司Dome Mine ，已经根据与McEwen的合资企业持有Paymaster的剩余40 ％股权。

此次撤资之际， McEwen正准备在其投资组合中将新矿投入生产。该公司的目标是到2030年达到每年25万至30万盎司的黄金当量，同时限制股东稀释。

McEwen计划将出售收益再投资于其运营矿山和开发项目，其安大略省业务仍专注于Froome、Stock和Grey Fox地产。

在福克斯综合体，库存矿预计将于2026年第四季度开始生产，其次是2027年第一季度的商业生产。

该公司还准备在内华达州的Grey Fox、Tartan和Gold Bar Complex进行地面工程和设备采购。

In Mexico, construction began at the El Gallo project in September, with production targeted for the second half of 2027.

The planned investments will test McEwen’s ability to expand output across several operations while pursuing its goal of funding growth with minimal new share issuance.
