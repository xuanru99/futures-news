Gold rebounds as traders position for Fed interest rate decision

FXStreet · 2026-09-16 19:28 · 机器翻译，仅供参考

追踪美元兑一篮子六种主要货币的美元指数（ DXY ）保持在99.65左右的两周高位附近，当天变化不大。基准10年期美国国债收益率在周二触及5.04%后接近4.99% ，为2007年以来的最高水平。

The US central bank will announce its decision at 18:00 GMT, followed by Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh’s press conference at 18:30 GMT. Markets widely expect a 25 basis-point rate hike, taking the federal funds target range to 3.75%-4.00%. If delivered, it would be the Fed’s first interest-rate increase since July 2023.

The anticipated rate increase reflects growing inflation risks from the energy shock caused by the war in the Middle East. With the United States and Iran still far from reaching a resolution and Oil prices holding at elevated levels, the Fed’s task of returning inflation to its 2% target has become more difficult. At the same time, the US labour market appears stable after a strong August employment report, giving policymakers more room to tighten policy.

荷兰国际集团（ ING ）的策略师预计，美联储将实现他们所谓的“共识25个基点”加息， “今天加息至4.0%” ，并指出“市场定价为今天为23个基点，年底为52个基点， 6月为89个基点。他们警告称，“意外持有可能会给美元带来巨大打击：无论是通过前端利率的鸽派重新定价，还是后端的可能抛售。”

In their view, this risk profile “also argues for a hawkish message,” as “a dovish hike may not be enough to convey the monetary policy discipline bond investors currently demand, particularly given the amount of tightening already priced into swaps.”

较高的借贷成本通常对黄金不利，因为它们增加了政府债券等计息资产的吸引力。然而，由于四分之一的涨幅几乎完全消除，金属的反应可能更多地取决于最新的经济预测摘要，包括点阵图和沃什的会后言论。

A hawkish Fed signal pointing to more rate increases could weigh on Gold. However, if the Fed avoids committing to another rate hike, the metal could build on its recovery.

黄金在人类历史上发挥了关键作用，因为它已被广泛用作价值储存和交换媒介。目前，除了其对珠宝的光泽和使用外，贵金属还被广泛视为避险资产，这意味着它被认为是动荡时期的良好投资。黄金也被广泛视为对冲通货膨胀和货币贬值的对冲工具，因为它不依赖于任何特定的发行人或政府。

中央银行是最大的黄金持有者。为了在动荡时期支持本国货币，各国央行倾向于分散储备并购买黄金，以提高经济和货币的感知实力。高黄金储备可以成为一国偿付能力的信任来源。根据世界黄金协会的数据，各国央行在2022年增加了1136吨黄金，价值约700亿美元。这是自有记录以来的最高年度购买量。中国、印度和土耳其等新兴经济体的央行正在迅速增加黄金储备。

Gold has an inverse correlation with the US Dollar and US Treasuries, which are both major reserve and safe-haven assets. When the Dollar depreciates, Gold tends to rise, enabling investors and central banks to diversify their assets in turbulent times. Gold is also inversely correlated with risk assets. A rally in the stock market tends to weaken Gold price, while sell-offs in riskier markets tend to favor the precious metal.

The price can move due to a wide range of factors. Geopolitical instability or fears of a deep recession can quickly make Gold price escalate due to its safe-haven status. As a yield-less asset, Gold tends to rise with lower interest rates, while higher cost of money usually weighs down on the yellow metal. Still, most moves depend on how the US Dollar (USD) behaves as the asset is priced in dollars (XAU/USD). A strong Dollar tends to keep the price of Gold controlled, whereas a weaker Dollar is likely to push Gold prices up.
