(2nd LD)特朗普公布韩国对阿拉斯加液化天然气项目、德克萨斯州发电厂、8座核反应堆的投资计划

WASHINGTON, Sept. 30 (Yonhap) -- U.S. President Donald Trump on Wednesday unveiled South Korea's U.S. investment projects under last year's bilateral trade agreement: the construction of a gas-fired power plant in Texas, eight large-scale nuclear power plants and a liquefied natural gas (LNG) project in Alaska.

根据该协议，韩国承诺在美国投资3500亿美元，其中1500亿美元用于造船业，其余用于战略投资，以换取美国对韩国商品降低关税。

Trump's explanation of the first investment package under the agreement suggested apparent differences with Seoul's position that only the Texas project has been finalized, while the Alaska LNG and nuclear projects remain subject to further discussions with the Trump administration.

特朗普强调了投资项目，因为他强调了他在11月中期选举之前的经济成就，公众担心与伊朗长达数月的战争导致高能源价格和经济不确定性加剧。

"(South Korea) will invest up to $200 billion ... to build a 6-gigawatt power generation facility in Texas. That's a major plant. That's about as big as you will see, (and) eight new large-scale nuclear power plants," Trump said at the White House.

"And at long last, Alaska liquefied natural gas pipeline. That's a big one," he added.

U.S. President Donald Trump makes an announcement with Secretary of Commerce Howard Lutnick in the Oval Office of the White House in Washington on Sept. 30, 2026, in this photo released by UPI. (Yonhap)

Trump touted the set of three projects as "the colossal package," saying it marked a "major step towards securing our critical energy supply chain, ensuring American energy dominance and making our partnership with South Korea and the entire Pacific region stronger than ever before."

“将在德克萨斯州建造的天然气发电厂将是世界上有史以来建造的最大的天然气发电厂之一。在全国范围内建造的八座新核反应堆将巩固我们作为世界领先核电生产国的地位，”总统说。

他补充说： “通过建设阿拉斯加液化天然气管道，我们每天将输送近40亿立方英尺的液化天然气，这使我们处于无法与其他任何国家相比的水平。”

The LNG project involves the construction of a 1,298-kilometer-long pipeline from the North Slope, which has massive proven natural gas reserves, to southern Alaska to transport natural gas that will be cooled to liquid form for shipments to Asia.

Trump made the announcement alongside Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, Interior Secretary Doug Burgum, Energy Secretary Chris Wright and Sen. Dan Sullivan (R-AK), calling the investment plans "one of the largest energy infrastructure investments in American history."

Lutnick said that over $50 billion will be invested in the Alaska LNG project, while $120 billion, including a $20 billion "contingency" fund, will be funneled into the project to build eight "gigantic" nuclear power plants across the United States, including in Ohio, Tennessee, South Carolina and Kentucky.

The secretary cast the Texas gas-fired power plant as a project worth over $22 billion.

去年10月，韩国总统李在铭和特朗普在韩国峰会期间达成了贸易协议。作为对首尔投资承诺的回报，华盛顿同意将其目前无效的韩国商品“互惠”关税从25%降至15%。

South Korea and the U.S. had engaged in difficult negotiations over the selection of investment projects amid Seoul's efforts to ensure "commercial viability" in those projects.

美国总统唐纳德·特朗普（ R ）于2026年9月30日在华盛顿白宫椭圆形办公室聆听商务部长霍华德·拉特尼克（ L ）的讲话，这张照片由法新社发布。（韩联社）
