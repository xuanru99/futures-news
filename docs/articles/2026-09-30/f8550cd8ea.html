AMD首席执行官下个月将访问韩国：消息来源

Yonhap · 2026-09-30 07:18 · 机器翻译，仅供参考

SEOUL, Sept. 30 (Yonhap) -- Lisa Su, chief executive officer of Advanced Micro Devices Inc. (AMD), is expected to visit South Korea next month to meet with South Korean semiconductor companies and government officials, industry sources said Wednesday.

The visit would come about seven months after her previous trip to South Korea in March.

据政府和半导体行业消息人士透露，苏正在考虑10月8日在首尔举行一次由苏出席的会议。

The meeting is expected to include the CEOs of major South Korean artificial intelligence (AI) semiconductor companies, including Rebellions Inc., FuriosaAI and Mobilint.

Su is also expected to meet with Science Minister Bae Kyung-hoon around the time of the meeting, the sources said.

在访问期间，苏还负责协调与韩国主要公司负责人的会晤，包括三星电子公司。

当她在3月访问时， AMD首席执行官会见了三星电子董事长Lee Jae-yong。

自2025年以来，三星电子一直向AMD提供HBM3E芯片，用于其最新的人工智能解决方案，两家公司保持着密切的业务关系。
