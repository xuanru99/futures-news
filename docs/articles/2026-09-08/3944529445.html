Op-Ed: The Lobito blind spot and Zambia’s manganese future

Mining · 2026-09-08 22:29 · 机器翻译，仅供参考

有一句关于“抢劫彼得以支付保罗”的古老谚语，基本上与修复一个区域而牺牲另一个区域有关。

Right now, Zambia’s critical mineral strategy is walking straight into this trap. The Lobito Corridor is currently the crown jewel of global energy transition projects. Backed by the US, the EU and G7 partners, it is being hyped everywhere. And for good reason, too.

其逻辑是，这条新走廊将绕过旧的瓶颈路线，穿过中非铜带到安哥拉的大西洋沿岸，并为西方锁定安全的铜和钴供应线。这是华盛顿对“一带一路”倡议下中国数十年物流主导地位的旗舰基础设施回应。

But every monolith casts a massive shadow. By hyper-focusing this multi-billion-dollar transit spine almost entirely on copper, policymakers are completely blind to a quiet giant whose demand is about to explode: manganese.

The tendency to think of manganese as simply a steel ingredient is becoming dangerously outdated. It already fortifies aerospace technology, automotive builds and offshore energy equipment. However, its true blockbuster era is happening right now in advanced energy storage. From lithium-manganese battery chemistries to emerging supercapacitor-battery hybrid systems, the metal is becoming increasingly central to the very technologies that prompted the creation of the Lobito Corridor in the first place.

中国最近调试了世界上第一个500兆瓦/1,000兆瓦时规模的超级电容器-电池混合储能设施，这是对市场可能走向的早期一瞥。随着这些技术蓝图扩展到大规模的商业现实，锰将不再由传统的烟囱行业驱动。它将为能源转型本身提供动力，赞比亚坐落在它的绝对金矿上。问题在于其基础设施是否准备就绪。

The fundamental flaw is that the Lobito Corridor was drawn to mirror only one type of geology. It neatly strings together the copper-heavy hubs of northwestern Zambia and southern DRC straight to the Port of Lobito. Mines in Solwezi, Kansanshi and Ndola sit comfortably on this luxury transport spine. This copper-centric alignment makes perfect sense since copper is a high-value commodity. Even before refining, it can absorb substantial transport costs while remaining commercially competitive. Every improvement in logistics strengthens an already viable — and politically visible — industry.

但赞比亚的锰讲述了一个非常不同的地质故事。它的主要锰矿主要位于曼萨周围的卢阿普拉省，以及中央省部分地区的重要矿床。这些地区位于现在吸引国际基础设施融资的特权交通脊柱之外。其结果是一个明显的不对称，在政策图上很容易错过，但在采矿经济学中却不容忽视。

Unlike copper, manganese is fundamentally a bulk commodity with a much lower value-to-weight ratio. A mine’s profitability is determined less by the quality of the orebody than by the cost of moving that ore hundreds or thousands of kilometres to market. For copper, logistics creates a competitive advantage, but for manganese, logistics tends to determine whether production remains viable at all.

这就是图片变得更有趣的地方。关键优势机会指数（ CDOI ）是一个框架，旨在确定各国可以在关键矿产价值链中现实地建立战略优势，它讲述了一个与传统基础设施规划不同的故事。

铜占据有利地位，特别是在赞比亚。全球铜加工仍然相对具有竞争力，这意味着拥有丰富资源和改善基础设施的国家仍然可以建立具有竞争力的下游产业。洛比托走廊通过降低运输成本和加强进一步投资的商业理由来加强这一机会。

锰呈现出相反的画面。其全球加工市场已经高度集中，这使得新进入者极难建立具有国际竞争力的炼油行业。我在之前发表的文章中对此进行了更详细的探讨。然而，这正是机会出现的地方。由于加工在结构上已经变得封闭，采矿是价值链中的最后一个主要点，各国仍然可以在这里建立战略优势。

The CDOI captures this contrast clearly. Copper offers opportunities in both production and processing, whereas manganese’s remaining strategic opening lies upstream, where mining remains comparatively contestable even as refining has consolidated. To put it bluntly, while Zambia is unlikely to become a global manganese refining hub, there is an enormous opportunity to become one of the world’s leading manganese producers before the next wave of battery demand reshapes global supply chains.

这改变了赞比亚对基础设施的看法。如果铜从加工走廊中获益最多，锰则从提取走廊中获益最多。这正是当前基础设施规划所忽视的区别。

To be clear, none of this means abandoning Lobito, but rather maximizing its strategic logic. Zambia’s own critical minerals strategy emphasizes exploration, local beneficiation and industrial value addition. Those ambitions require more than identifying mineral deposits. They require infrastructure that reflects the country’s full geological diversity rather than reinforcing the dominance of a single commodity.

升级Kapiri Mposhi周围的货运能力，将铁路连接扩展到锰产区，改善多式联运一体化以及围绕多种关键矿物而不是单一商品规划货运网络，将加强赞比亚的长期工业地位。

这些举动并不会夺走铜的一分钱；它们只是防止赞比亚成为一只被单一商品束缚的小马。具有讽刺意味的是，通过认识到赞比亚的比较优势超出了铜带本身，西方的旗舰关键矿产走廊可能会变得更加强大。

The real risk is not that Zambia is investing too much in copper, but that success in one critical mineral is quietly blinding policymakers to another. Infrastructure is never neutral. The Lobito Corridor shouldn’t be allowed to devolve into a rigid, copper-first echo chamber. If policymakers don’t widen their lens right now, Zambia risks spending billions building the perfect, state-of-the-art railway tailored entirely to yesterday’s priorities.

Nicholas Vafeas is the founder and director of BluMelt Mineral Consulting Limited, an independent consultancy specialising in geological assurance, critical minerals and investment de-risking.
