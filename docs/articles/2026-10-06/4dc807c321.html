（第4次LD ）第五次发射前夕在发射台上架设的Nuri火箭

（收件人：第4-5段包含最新详细信息的更新）作者： Kim Eun-jung

首尔， 10月6日（韩联社） -韩国航天局表示，韩国本土的Nuri火箭于周二被移动到发射台并升高到垂直位置，这是计划的第五次发射前一天。

The Korea AeroSpace Administration (KASA) said the launch team completed transporting the rocket to the launchpad at the Naro Space Center in Goheung, about 330 kilometers south of Seoul, at 7:30 a.m. after a 90-minute journey from the assembly complex.

据韩国航空航天研究所称，火箭是垂直架设的，并于上午10:35固定到位。

韩国航空航天研究所（ Korea Aerospace Research Institute ）提供的这张合成图像显示，韩国本土的Nuri火箭将于2026年10月6日在首尔东南约330公里的高兴（ Goheung ）纳罗航天中心（ Naro Space Center ）的发射台上竖立，比第五次发射提前一天。（照片不出售） （ Yonhap ）

The launch team connected the rocket to the umbilical systems, which supply power and other necessary connections, and conducted a series of checks in preparation for fueling, completing the work at 8:05 p.m.

KASA will convene a launch management committee at 8 a.m. Wednesday to determine the final launch time after reviewing the rocket's technical readiness, weather conditions and the possibility of collisions with space objects. The final launch time will be announced at 8:45 a.m.

韩国航空航天研究所（ Korea Aerospace Research Institute ）提供的这张照片显示， 2026年10月6日，韩国本土的Nuri火箭站在首尔东南约330公里处的高兴（ Goheung ）纳罗航天中心的发射台上，比第五次发射提前一天。（照片不出售） （ Yonhap ）

The payload consists of five New-space Earth Observation Satellite Constellation for National Safety (NEONSAT) microsatellites, which will form part of South Korea's first domestically launched microsatellite constellation, as well as 10 CubeSats.

第五次飞行将标志着Nuri首次尝试依次部署多颗旨在执行相同任务的主要卫星。

About four hours before liftoff on Wednesday, the launch team will begin filling the three-stage rocket with kerosene fuel and liquid oxygen oxidizer after completing system checks.

5颗NEONSAT卫星（ 2号至6号）将间隔35至40秒部署在海拔约575公里的地方。然后， 10个立方体卫星将以10秒的间隔每次部署两个。

The NEONSATs will conduct high-frequency monitoring of the Korean Peninsula and surrounding areas for national security and disaster response.

The constellation will eventually consist of 10 satellites, with another five to be launched aboard Nuri's sixth flight scheduled for next year.

Once fully operational, the constellation will be capable of imaging the Korean Peninsula more than three times a day and revisiting the same location within 24 hours.
