N. Korea reviews Q3 economy, discusses yearly goals in Cabinet meeting

Yonhap · 2026-10-06 07:33 · 机器翻译，仅供参考

SEOUL, Oct. 6 (Yonhap) -- North Korea has held an enlarged Cabinet meeting via video conference to review its third-quarter economic performances, state media reported Tuesday.

The meeting, presided over by Premier Pak Thae-song on Monday, also discussed ways to meet the year's economic targets, according to the Rodong Sinmun, the country's most widely read state newspaper.

官员们回顾了2月份举行的朝鲜劳动党第九次代表大会制定的关键目标，该大会制定了到2030年的新的五年计划，强调经济增长和自力更生。

According to the report, Vice Premier Kim Tok-hun said, "Remarkable progress has been made, and the foundation for self-reliant economy has been reinforced."

他还呼吁官员放弃“被动和规避风险”的工作态度，以克服在执行经济政策中发现的“缺陷和偏见”。

The meeting, in particular, called for production drives in electricity, coal, agriculture and construction. It urged work on the fifth phase of the Hwasong district, a newly developed residential district in Pyongyang, and on local industrial factories.
