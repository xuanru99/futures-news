Australian Dollar drifts higher above 0.6950 on soft US jobs data

FXStreet · 2026-10-05 07:21 · 机器翻译，仅供参考

Data released by the US Bureau of Labor Statistics on Friday showed that Nonfarm Payrolls rose by 29,000 jobs in September, followed by 133,000 in August (revised from 162,000). This figure came in below the market consensus of 90,000.

在美国就业数据疲软之后，对美联储本月晚些时候加息的预期有所下降。芝加哥商品交易所联邦观察工具(CME FedWatch Tool)的数据显示，市场现在预计10月份美联储加息的可能性接近22.1% ， 12月份加息的可能性为87.2%。

在澳大利亚方面，最新的消费者价格指数（ CPI ）符合预期后，澳大利亚储备银行（ RBA ） 11月加息的几率大幅下降。货币市场现在押注，澳大利亚央行可能会在11月的政策会议上保持利率不变。LSEG的数据显示，加息的可能性降至20%左右。

德国商业银行(Commerzbank)的分析师认为，最新数据强调了为什么“澳联储(RBA)加息1.5次--正如市场昨天仍在预期的那样--可能过高。“澳大利亚储备银行货币政策会议后一天，他们指出，今天公布的CPI数据“也表明”谨慎的理由，尽管“毫无疑问，通货膨胀仍然太高，d需要一段时间才能返回到目标范围的中间。”

Commerzbank stresses that “interest rate hikes always take effect with a certain time lag,” and points in particular to the real estate sector, “where building permits fell again in August by 6.1% compared to the previous month and prices in the largest cities continue to decline.” Against this backdrop, the bank judges that “the RBA would likely be well advised to wait and see how things develop in the coming months.” In turn, they conclude that “the AUD is unlikely to receive any further tailwind.”

Fed’s Logan delivered a notably more hawkish message, with the FXS Speechtracker score at 9.2/10, well above the 8.1/10 historical average, underscoring a stronger tightening bias relative to the established baseline. The emphasis that higher yields may reflect increased term premiums, potentially reducing the need for further aggressive tightening, sits alongside explicit calls for at least 50 bps more in rate hikes and several additional moves, reinforcing a view that policy is not yet restrictive enough and that inflation will not reach 2% without higher rates, a mix that is broadly supportive for the Dollar. Overall, the speech signals confidence in economic expansion and a balanced labor market, but with a clear priority on reviving price stability through additional tightening.

The FXS Fed Sentiment Index rose by 1.68 points to 136.59, confirming a solid move deeper into hawkish territory and aligning with the elevated FXS Speechtracker reading. With the index far above the neutral 100 mark, the data point reinforces expectations for further policy rate increases and keeps the Dollar underpinned against the Euro and Yen.

One of the most significant factors for the Australian Dollar (AUD) is the level of interest rates set by the Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA). Because Australia is a resource-rich country another key driver is the price of its biggest export, Iron Ore. The health of the Chinese economy, its largest trading partner, is a factor, as well as inflation in Australia, its growth rate and Trade Balance. Market sentiment – whether investors are taking on more risky assets (risk-on) or seeking safe-havens (risk-off) – is also a factor, with risk-on positive for AUD.

澳大利亚储备银行（ RBA ）通过设定澳大利亚银行可以相互借贷的利率水平来影响澳元（ AUD ）。这会影响整个经济的利率水平。澳大利亚央行的主要目标是通过上调或下调利率，将通货膨胀率维持在2-3%的稳定水平。与其他主要央行相比，相对较高的利率支持澳元，而相对较低的利率则相反。澳大利亚央行还可以使用量化宽松和紧缩来影响信贷状况，前者为负澳元，后者为正澳元。

China is Australia’s largest trading partner so the health of the Chinese economy is a major influence on the value of the Australian Dollar (AUD). When the Chinese economy is doing well it purchases more raw materials, goods and services from Australia, lifting demand for the AUD, and pushing up its value. The opposite is the case when the Chinese economy is not growing as fast as expected. Positive or negative surprises in Chinese growth data, therefore, often have a direct impact on the Australian Dollar and its pairs.

根据2021年的数据，铁矿石是澳大利亚最大的出口产品，每年出口额为1180亿美元，中国是其主要目的地。因此，铁矿石的价格可能是澳元的推动力。一般来说，如果铁矿石价格上涨，随着货币总需求的增加，澳元也会上涨。如果铁矿石价格下跌，情况正好相反。铁矿石价格上涨也往往会导致澳大利亚出现正贸易平衡的可能性更大，这也对澳元有利。

贸易平衡是另一个可能影响澳元价值的因素，贸易平衡是一个国家从出口中获得的收入与为进口支付的收入之间的差异。如果澳大利亚出口受到高度追捧，那么其货币将纯粹从寻求购买其出口品的外国买家相对于购买进口品的支出所产生的剩余需求中获益。因此，正的净贸易差额会加强澳元，如果贸易差额为负，则会产生相反的效果。
