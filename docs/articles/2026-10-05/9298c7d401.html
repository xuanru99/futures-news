The share of gasoline cars in global vehicle sales fell below 50% for the first time ever as the Hormuz shock and the resulting record-high fuel prices accelerated the previously sluggish shift toward EVs outside China.

几年前，中国超越了混合动力汽车和电动汽车占所有汽车销量50%的门槛，现在的份额约为55%。但直到伊朗战争扰乱了燃料市场，世界其他国家才跟进。

电动汽车的全球销售份额与中国的数据相去甚远，但在汽油和柴油价格飙升的情况下，向电动汽车的转变速度非常快，这促使许多消费者选择电动汽车。

The surge in gasoline prices this year led to the first dip in the share of gasoline-fueled cars below 50%, ever, according to data by automotive data and analytics provider Mobility Global cited by Nikkei Asia.

Between January and June, sales of gas-powered vehicles, excluding hybrids, slumped by 10% from a year earlier, to 20.25 million cars, and their share of total global automotive sales fell by 3 percentage points to 49%, the data showed. 多年来，中国以外市场的电动汽车普及率低迷且预测滞后，但霍尔木兹危机、100 $油价以及创纪录的柴油和汽油价格促使欧洲、南美和亚太地区的许多消费者转向电动汽车。

Battery electric vehicle sales in Europe, including the UK, Switzerland, and Norway, surged by 52.2% in August from a year earlier as record-high gasoline and diesel prices prompted drivers to flock to battery and hybrid vehicles, data by the European Automobile Manufacturers’ Association, ACEA, showed last month.

8月份，欧洲最大的汽车市场德国的BEV销量同比飙升75% ，德国汽油价格创下每升2.31欧元的历史新高，相当于每加仑约10 $。

另一个主要的欧洲市场法国的电动汽车销量增长了一倍以上。

In Germany, where the government introduced a purchase premium for EVs from January 2026, sales are accelerating, an analysis by the German Institute for Economic Research, DIW, showed earlier this week.

根据分析， 1月至8月期间在德国销售的每四辆乘用车中就有一辆是纯电动汽车，而8月份的份额更高--每三辆乘用车中就有一辆是纯电动汽车。相比之下， 2025年的平均水平为19%。

在全球范围内，继中东石油供应中断和四年来的第二次油价冲击之后，今年电动汽车销量飙升。

国际能源署（ IEA ）在7月份更新其年度电动汽车前景时表示，在中东危机削减原油供应并提高石油和燃料价格之后，电动汽车销量在第二季度出现飙升。

EV sales rebounded sharply in the second quarter, rising by 35% compared with the first quarter “as the energy crisis sparked by the war in the Middle East brought fuel price volatility back into sharp focus,” the agency said.

EV sales in the second quarter reached record-high levels in 50 countries as drivers preferred electric vehicles amid soaring fuel prices.

In sizable car markets such as Brazil, India, Australia, and Vietnam, electric car sales roughly doubled between March and June compared with the same period in 2025, according to the IEA’s analysis in the report.

Moreover, as many as 90 countries saw annual growth in EV sales in the first half of the year.

Wood Mackenzie的分析师表示，随着石油和燃料价格飙升，电动汽车的加速采用将继续成为全球市场的趋势，并可能推动电动汽车在乘用车队中的份额超过早先的预期。

加速采用的挑战仍然存在，包括需要在关键电池矿物供应和充电网络上投资数十亿美元。然而，霍尔木兹海峡危机扰乱全球燃料市场的时间越长，采用电动汽车的理由就越强烈。
