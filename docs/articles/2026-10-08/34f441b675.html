(LEAD) S. Korea extends current account surplus in Aug. amid solid exports

Yonhap · 2026-10-08 07:19 · 机器翻译，仅供参考

世宗， 10月8日（韩联社） -韩国央行周四表示，由于全球半导体上升周期中出口强劲，韩国8月份出现经常账户盈余，创下有史以来的第二高纪录。

根据韩国央行（ BOK ）的数据， 8月份经常账户盈余总额为461亿美元，比一个月前增加了40亿美元。

Compared with the same month in 2025, the figure jumped $36.2 billion.

It marked the second-largest figure, following $49.73 billion set in June this year.

The figure remained above the $40 billion mark for the third consecutive month. In July, it stood at $42 billion.

在1月至8月期间，经常账户盈余总额为2792 $ ，比去年同期几乎翻了两番。

The BOK said the goods account posted a surplus of $46.81 billion in August, as exports shot up 82.1 percent on-year to $104.8 billion in the month, while imports rose 23.8 percent to $57.99 billion.

Exports of chips more than tripled on a customs-clearance basis, while those of petroleum products gained 64.9 percent, the BOK report showed. Outbound shipments of cars and ships, meanwhile, fell 30.1 percent and 45.2 percent, respectively.

该银行表示，该服务账户在8月份录得16.8亿美元的赤字，主要是由于其他商业服务和旅行方面的赤字。

在服务账户中，差旅赤字从7月份的3.4亿美元扩大到7.7亿美元。

The central bank added the primary income account, which covers wages of foreign workers, as well as dividend and interest income from abroad, logged a $1.92 billion surplus.

自2023年5月以来，韩国每月都报告经常账户盈余，使8月连续第40个月的余额保持在黑色。

In 2025, the country posted its largest annual surplus on record at $123.05 billion, exceeding the previous high of $105.1 billion set in 2015.
