Who Is Going to Pay for Alaska LNG?

Natalia Katona is a freelance commodity analyst, based in the United Arab Emirates.

Washington’s tariff pressure is giving Alaska LNG something its economics have struggled to secure: a potential investor. Discussed for decades, the costly and technically complex project has regained momentum under President Donald Trump, who is using tariff threats to press foreign partners into backing it. South Korea is now at the centre of that effort, as Seoul’s bid to protect access to the US market becomes a negotiation over financing one of America’s most expensive energy developments.

The question is whether that pressure can produce a commercially viable investment. Washington’s threat of 25% tariffs on Korean exports led to an arrangement, lowering the rate to 15% in exchange for $350 billion in investments and $100 billion in US energy purchases. South Korea’s parliament passed corresponding legislation in March 2026, with negotiations moving toward specific energy projects in September.

On October 1, Seoul outlined potential investments including a 6.47 GW gas-fired power project in Texas serving AI data centres from 2029, and eight US nuclear reactors costing a combined $120 billion, including two using Korea’s APR-1400 design. Alaska LNG was also under review, conditional on commercial and legal requirements being met and that condition is what matters. In September, President Trump had already announced that South Korea was to pay more than $50 billion toward Alaska LNG at an event attended by Alaska officials and Glenfarne Chief Executive Brendan Duval. Seoul’s conditional review falls well short of that commitment.

拟议的450亿至540亿美元开发项目由Glenfarne拥有75% ，由阿拉斯加州拥有25% ，将把North Slope天然气输送给国内消费者，并最终在阿拉斯加中南部的Nikiski建立一个出口终端。然而，该项目有一个根本的缺点，那就是将远程储备连接到客户的巨大成本：处理设施和大约1,300公里（ 807英里）的管道必须先于出口收入。

项目的地理位置是主要问题。大部分管道将被掩埋在艰难的地形中，包括转移的永久冻土和高达1.5公里（ 5,000英尺）的山地过境点。大约54%的窗户只能在夏季建造，因此错过建造窗户是一个很大的延误风险。未解决的国家税收安排和国内成本分配增加了谁吸收可能超支的不确定性，包括阿拉斯加家庭是否会面临更高的燃气和电费。阿拉斯加必须建设极其昂贵的基础设施，以达到竞争出口商的出发点。

Capital intensity approaches $2.7 billion per million tonnes of annual LNG capacity (in comparison to an $898 million/tonne for Qatar’s North Field East and $762 million/tonne for Cheniere’s Corpus Christi Stage 3). Development would proceed in phases, beginning with the pipeline that would feed the local market before the larger export component (first exports are targeted for 2031). This means that financing and cost recovery during the domestic-supply phase is yet another complication for the matter.

Asian commitments have so far been considerably narrower than Trump’s announcements. In July 2025, he said Japan would form an Alaska LNG joint venture under its $550 billion US investment vehicle. Alaska LNG subsequently disappeared from Japan’s list of potential investments. Japanese participation instead centred on an autumn 2025 letter of intent involving Jera and Tokyo Gas to purchase 1 million tonnes of LNG annually for 20 years.

韩国浦项制铁国际同样签署了一项不具约束力的协议，在20年内每年购买100万吨管道钢，并提供大量管道钢。台湾、泰国和道达尔能源也表达了潜在的购买意向。此类安排表明需求，但不结算施工融资或风险分配。

尽管如此，韩国仍有充分理由继续获得美国液化天然气。其每年4780万吨的需求使Posco拟议的阿拉斯加采购相形见绌。2025年，美国供应了440万吨，落后于澳大利亚的1470万吨、马来西亚的790万吨和卡塔尔的720万吨。在2月下旬美伊冲突开始后，这种平衡发生了变化，卡塔尔实际上停止了液化天然气的出口。韩国从美国的进口量从3月份的20万吨（总计310万吨）增加到7月份的100万吨（总计370万吨）。美国与澳大利亚一起成为领先的供应商。

Yet greater reliance on US supply coincides with weakening Korean LNG demand. Net-zero policies are gradually reducing consumption, while wartime price increases prompted temporarily higher coal-fired generation and subsequent lower gas burning. Over the summer months, South Korea’s gas-fired power generation fell 9% year-over-year to an average 13 GWh. For reference, Korean coal usage is so far up 18% in 2026 to date, averaging almost 15 GWh throughout January-July. Milder summer temperatures also softened electricity demand during the usual summer and early-autumn peak.

在与美国液化天然气的欧洲买家竞争时，阿拉斯加提供了切实的优势。美国墨西哥湾沿岸在好望角周围运往韩国的货物大约需要40–45天，目前的运费估计约为42,000 $/天。巴拿马提供较短的路线，但即使与好望角路线相比，过境限制、排队和运河收费也使其不那么有吸引力。相比之下，尼基斯基到朝鲜的可能航行大约需要10–12天，避免了运河和主要咽喉。

然而，韩国已经可以在不为阿拉斯加管道提供资金的情况下获得太平洋液化天然气。由壳牌、马来西亚国家石油公司、中国石油、三菱和韩国自己的KOGAS支持的LNG Canada于2025年7月开始出口，今年9月已向亚洲市场出口了100万吨。它即将扩张到每年2800万吨（上月底批准） ，这将增加对阿拉斯加买家需求的竞争。较短的航程是有价值的，但阿拉斯加并不垄断它们。

因此，首尔的谨慎早于当前的谈判。去年，韩国贸易和工业部长将阿拉斯加液化天然气描述为高风险和不可行的投资选择。然而，关税压力使得在外交上重复这一判断变得更加困难，而没有解决潜在的经济学问题。

For now, Seoul will most likely try to gain time before making a diplomatically risky announcement of not participating in the project. Trump’s signalling of Korean investment into an unspecified $8.4 billion enhanced oil recovery project could offer this alternative. Such projects use injections of steam, gases or chemicals to extract additional crude. Too few details are available to assess this proposal, but it could give Seoul another way to demonstrate investment cooperation while limiting exposure to the commercially questionable Alaska LNG project.
