李克强将与埃及总统举行经济合作峰会

Yonhap · 2026-10-08 07:37 · 机器翻译，仅供参考

SEOUL, Oct. 8 (Yonhap) -- President Lee Jae Myung was set Thursday to hold a summit with Egyptian President Abdel Fattah El-Sisi in Seoul to discuss ways to expand economic cooperation between the two countries.

El-Sisi arrived in South Korea the previous day on a three-day state visit, his first trip to the country since 2016.

Following an official welcoming ceremony, the two leaders will hold summit talks, attend an agreement signing ceremony, a joint press briefing and a state luncheon, according to Cheong Wa Dae.

预计双方将讨论贸易投资、基础设施和供应链等经济合作，以及扩大人文交流、地区政治局势和国际问题。

"We expect President El-Sisi's state visit to serve as an important opportunity to develop South Korea-Egypt ties into a strategic partnership and further strengthen our country's cooperation with Africa and the Middle East," presidential spokesperson Kang Yu-jung said earlier.

The two leaders last met in November during Lee's visit to Egypt.
