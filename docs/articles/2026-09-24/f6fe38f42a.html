SEOUL, Sept. 24 (Yonhap) -- Russia has officially invited North Korean leader Kim Jong-un to visit the country, a Russian deputy foreign minister said, according to the TASS news agency Thursday.

该机构表示，访问时间表将通过外交渠道进行协调。

俄罗斯总统弗拉基米尔·普京（ Vladimir Putin ）在2024年6月与朝鲜领导人在平壤举行峰会后邀请金正恩访问俄罗斯，并在两位领导人去年9月在中国举行的阅兵式上再次会晤时提出了同样的建议。

Speculation that Kim could visit Moscow within this year grew after North Korean Foreign Minister Choe Son-hui made an unexpected visit to Russia in July at the invitation of Russia's foreign ministry.

Kim has visited Russia twice, in 2019 and 2023, but met Putin on both occasions in Russia's Far East. He has never visited Moscow.

Pyongyang and Moscow have deepened their alignment since North Korea deployed troops to support Russia's war against Ukraine.
