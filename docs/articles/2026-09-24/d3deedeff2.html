TOKYO, Sept. 24 (Yonhap) -- South Korea claimed silver in the men's 4x100-meter freestyle relay swimming at the Asian Games on Thursday, its second straight runner-up finish.

Hwang Sun-woo、Kim Ji-hun、Yang Jae-hoon和Kim Young-beom在东京水上运动中心发布了3:11.69的时间，落后中国1.09秒，创下了3:10.60的亚洲纪录。

Kim Woo-min因肩伤参加比赛。周一，他在男子4x200米自由泳接力赛中帮助韩国队赢得了铜牌，但随后在周二的1500米自由泳比赛中获得最后一名，之后他透露，他的左肩上有一个部分撕裂的唇骨和旋转袖口。

He pulled out of the 200m and 800m freestyle races set for Wednesday and Thursday, and chose only to compete in the relay heats Thursday. After the relay, Kim decided to also withdraw from the 400m freestyle, his main event, scheduled for Friday.

Kim Young-beom感谢Woo-min和其他所有在热火中游泳的人，并透露他在接力决赛中戴着Woo-min的帽子。

"We posted a great time here. It may look slow next to the Chinese team's time, but we were really fast today," Kim Young-beom said. "I had a blast. This was a super fun race."

Kim collected five medals in Tokyo, including gold in the men's 50m freestyle.
