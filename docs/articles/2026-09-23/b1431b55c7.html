Watchdog chief calls for fairness, transparency in CEO selection process

Yonhap · 2026-09-23 06:40 · 机器翻译，仅供参考

SEOUL, Sept. 23 (Yonhap) -- The chief of the country's financial watchdog said Wednesday that bank holding companies need to draw up measures to boost fairness and transparency in selecting chief executives of their affiliates.

在与八家银行控股公司负责人的会晤中，金融监管局（ FSS ）局长Lee Chan-jin敦促他们加倍努力，以公平和透明的方式遴选其关联公司的首席执行官。

Financial Supervisory Service (FSS) Gov. Lee Chan-jin (Yonhap)

监管机构一直在竞相制定措施，以促进金融控股公司的治理结构，这些结构以公平和透明的银行首席执行官遴选为中心。

Lee还强调，随着金融事故的稳步上升，银行需要加强内部控制计划。

According to the FSS, a total of 171 billion won (US$126 million) worth of financial accidents were reported in the banking sector in the first seven months of the year, sharply up from 69.6 billion won in 2023.
