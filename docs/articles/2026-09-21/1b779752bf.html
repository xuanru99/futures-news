NAGOYA, Japan, Sept. 20 (Yonhap) -- No one scored more points, made more three-pointers, grabbed more rebounds or had more steals in the entire tournament. Considering the stakes, it would not be an exaggeration to say Lee Hyun-jung played the best basketball of his life during his six-game run to the gold medal for South Korea at the Asian Games this month.

Lee poured in 27 points and grabbed 18 boards to help South Korea beat Japan 67-57 for the gold medal at Aichi International Arena in Nagoya, Japan, on Sunday, the country's first men's hoops gold since 2014.

Lee finished with 144 total points in six games. He drained 25 shots from downtown, with no one else even over 20, and also had 14 steals. Despite not being a big man, the 202-centimeter forward tied for the tournament lead with 58 rebounds.

2026年9月20日在日本名古屋举行的爱知国际体育馆亚运会男子篮球比赛中赢得金牌后，韩国（ R ）的Lee Hyun-jung高五球迷。（韩联社）

Lee shot 52 percent from the field, 47 percent from behind the arc, and 84 percent from the charity stripe. Even in a small, six-game sample, these are gaudy numbers that can turn what has mostly been a mediocre team into a tournament champion.

"I just shot when I had a chance and went after the rebounds," Lee said during a mixed-zone interview. "I know Japan's Alex Kirk is a strong rebounder, but he is a little slow in transition after losing a rebound. So I thought it would be easier if I went after more rebounds."

He is now the second member of his family with an Asian Games gold, after his mother, former center Seong Jeong-a, lifted South Korea to the women's title at the 1990 competition in Beijing.

"She didn't say much to me. She just told me to be myself in a calm, cool-headed way," Lee said. "I think I am very lucky that she is my mother. She has helped me become a better player while teaching me to remain humble."

25岁的李还带来了大量无形资产作为领导者。他几乎不回避低位篮板的身体战斗，并做了一些聪明的插座通行证，开始快速休息。在整个锦标赛中， Lee鼓吹冷静和镇定，并坚称直到决赛的最后一声蜂鸣器才会完成这项工作，而他的一些队友可能因为对赢得金牌的前景过于兴奋而感到内疚。

34岁的队长Lee Seoung-hyun甚至表示，他很荣幸能在国家队与像Lee Hyun-jung这样才华横溢、谦逊的球员一起比赛，并称他为未来的队长材料。

And Lee will get to enjoy his national team and professional career without interruption, after earning exemption from the mandatory military service with this gold medal.

“我认为这枚金牌很重要，因为我想在全球舞台上走得更远，”他说。“但我不认为这是我的国家队生涯的结束。我会不断努力，不辜负粉丝的期望。”

Lee attended Davidson, best known as alma mater for National Basketball Association (NBA) superstar Stephen Curry. As a sophomore, Lee accomplished something that even Curry never did -- shooting 50.8 percent from the field, 44.2 percent from the three-point range and 90 percent from the free throw line for the coveted 50-40-90 combination.

李在2022年宣布参加NBA选秀，但在选秀前几天脚部受伤。此后，他曾在澳大利亚和日本打球，并在二线NBA G联赛中短暂出场。

今年夏天，他在NBA夏季联赛中为圣安东尼奥马刺队效力，后来参加了波士顿凯尔特人和新奥尔良鹈鹕队的迷你训练营。然后在亚运会之前， Lee与波特兰开拓者队签订了一份展览10合同。这是一项为期一年的非保证协议，提供最低工资并邀请参加训练营。

随着下周NBA训练营的开放， Lee在这里帮助自己赢得了金牌。他现在将努力成为继前中锋Ha Seung-jin之后的第二位在NBA打球的韩国人。

“我想做的事情太多了。我想参加世界杯、奥运会和NBA ，”他说。“我想听到人们说韩国篮球不是弱，而是强。”
