(Asiad) Freestyle swimmer feels extra motivated after failing to defend title

Yonhap · 2026-09-21 19:38 · 机器翻译，仅供参考

TOKYO, Sept. 21 (Yonhap) -- Ji Yu-chan was hoping to become the first swimmer to win back-to-back Asian Games gold medals in the men's 50-meter freestyle race on Monday.

相反，他不得不满足于连续第二枚奖牌，因为他在东京水上运动中心以21.94秒的成绩获得铜牌。

Fellow Korean Kim Young-beom grabbed the gold with a new Asian record of 21.66 seconds, and Ho Ian Yentou of Hong Kong won silver in 21.76 seconds.

韩国的Ji Yu-chan在2026年9月21日在东京东京水上运动中心举行的亚运会男子50米自由泳比赛中获得铜牌。（韩联社）

"My goal was to win my second straight gold. It didn't happen, but I am still happy with the result. Considering how much I struggled before this competition, I am really satisfied here," Ji said. "At least Young-beom finished first and I got to hear our national anthem at the ceremony. So that meant a lot."

1990年，男子50米自由泳成为亚运会的一项运动。在姬和2023年银牌得主何周一完成这一壮举之前，甚至没有人连续赢得两枚奖牌。

"Chasing a milestone like that gave me motivation," Ji said. "Now, I really want to go for another medal at the next Asian Games. It has been a while since I came under 22 seconds, and I think I can sleep easy tonight."

纪以21.72秒的成绩夺得2023年亚运会冠军。Kim首先在周一的比赛中打破了0.01秒，然后在决赛中进一步提高到21.66秒。

“我真的希望Young-beom能赢得金牌，如果不是我，” Ji说。“他今天看起来很棒，我有一种感觉，他会完成它。所以我只是想和他分享领奖台，我很高兴结果是这样。”
