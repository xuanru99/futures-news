在议会简报会之前，华盛顿首尔仍在就投资项目进行艰难的谈判

Yonhap · 2026-09-21 19:16 · 机器翻译，仅供参考

SEOUL, Sept. 21 (Yonhap) -- South Korea and the United States still have to iron out a string of issues on terms of Seoul's investment project in the world's largest economy ahead of the South Korean government's parliamentary briefing, political sources said Monday.

Seoul and Washington have been in negotiations over the first investment project under South Korea's US$350 billion investment commitment to the U.S.

The government is expected to brief on negotiation results and other details for parliamentary committees on Tuesday.

South Korea has focused on "commercial rationality" in seeking its first mega investment project in the U.S., but Washington is said to have been pushing for a speedy conclusion.

在去年李在铭总统和美国总统唐纳德·特朗普举行峰会之后，首尔和华盛顿通过了一份联合概况介绍。根据协议，韩国承诺在美国投资3500亿美元（ $ 3500亿美元） ，包括造船合作1500亿美元（ $ 1500亿美元） ，以换取美国降低关税。

在上周四政府向国民议会通报情况后，最初预计两国将在周五宣布第一个项目，但由于继续消除分歧，该公告被推迟。

预计政府将为潜在能源项目提供相对详细的资金规模和分配计划，包括德克萨斯州的大型燃气发电厂和核电项目。

消息人士称，双方在如何确保“商业可行性”方面仍存在分歧，韩国正寻求将其作为其投资承诺的一部分进行担保。

The Seoul government is seeking safeguards that would allow it to adjust or halt investments if projects prove economically unviable or unexpected risks arise, while also calling for an annual cap on investment transfers.

The U.S., however, has reportedly shown reluctance toward Seoul's proposed safeguards and is pressing for some projects to move forward more quickly.

Foreign Minister Cho Hyun told reporters Friday (U.S. time) following his talks with U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio in Washington that they agreed to continue communicating closely to implement key agreements between the two leaders.

美国国务院表示，卢比奥强调需要迅速履行投资承诺。
