(LEAD) (Asiad) S. Korea claims bronze in 4x200m freestyle relay swimming

东京， 9月21日（韩联社） -韩国周一在亚运会游泳比赛中赢得了男子4x200米自由泳接力比赛的铜牌，距离冠军防守不远。

The quartet of Kim Jun-woo, Kim Woo-min, Lee Ho-joon and Hwang Sun-woo combined for a time of 7:06.76, finishing in third place at Tokyo Aquatics Centre in Tokyo, as China broke its own Asian record with 7:00.17. Japan, after leading most of the way, settled for silver in 7:01.05.

Kim Jun-woo, Kim Woo-min, Lee Ho-joon and Hwang Sun-woo (L to R) of South Korea pose with their bronze medals won in the men's 4x200-meter freestyle relay swimming race at the Asian Games at Tokyo Aquatics Centre in Tokyo on Sept. 21, 2026. (Yonhap)

Kim Woo-min, Lee and Hwang had won the 2023 Asian Games gold together with a different fourth member, Yang Jae-hoon, with a then continental record of 7:01.73.

China broke that mark last year, clocking 7:00.91, and it bettered that record by another 0.74 second on Monday.

这枚奖牌为韩国赢得了一枚金牌和四枚铜牌，在泳池中取得了丰硕的第2天成绩。

Hwang和Kim Woo-min没有参加接力加热， Yang和Kim Min-seop取而代之。Yang和Kim Min-seop也获得了奖牌。

Kim Jun-woo led off the final with an opening split of 1:47.16, putting South Korea 2.46 seconds behind Japan in third place. And South Korea stayed in third place for the remainder of the race.

Kim Woo-min以1:46.16紧随其后， Lee以1:47.57盖住他的腿，让韩国领先5.53秒，没有实际机会捍卫自己的冠军。作为主播，黄在1:45.87完成了最后一回合，比中国主播张占硕慢了近三整秒，张占硕帮助他的球队在主场大举复出。

The four South Korean swimmers all wore a despondent look afterward, unhappy with both the color of the medal and the time.

"I am happy that we won our second straight medal, but at the same time, I am a bit disappointed with the outcome," Kim Woo-min said. "We wanted to defend our title, but we just did not get it done."

Kim Jun-woo, the youngest member of the team at 19, said he was honored to have competed alongside the veterans in his Asian Games debut.

"I learned so much from this experience," he said. "I hope to use this as a launching pad and develop into a more competitive swimmer."

Lee和Hwang都表示，尽管他们对第一次接力赛感到失望，但他们希望为剩下的比赛做好准备。

"I felt we were the underdogs coming in, even though we were the defending champions," Hwang added. "Chinese and Japanese swimmers were peaking for this event, and I don't think we were quite able to match them here. Hopefully, we will do better in upcoming relays."
