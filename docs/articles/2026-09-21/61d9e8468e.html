Big Oil’s Production Keeps Soaring Despite Deep Spending Cuts

OilPrice · 2026-09-21 07:00 · 机器翻译，仅供参考

Macron Calls for Another Emergency Oil Release as Europe Loses Supply

Alex Kimani是Safehaven.com的资深金融作家、投资者、工程师和研究员。

自2020年历史性的油价暴跌摧毁了能源公司以来，一些全球最大的石油和天然气公司已经采用了一种新的运作方式，优先考虑向股东返还更多现金，而扩张计划却被搁置一边。事实上，在过去五年中，埃克森美孚（纽约证券交易所代码： XOM ）、雪佛龙（纽约证券交易所代码： CVX ）、英国石油公司（纽约证券交易所代码： BP ）、壳牌（纽约证券交易所代码： SHEL ）和道达尔能源公司（纽约证券交易所代码： TTE ）每年共花费超过1000亿美元用于股息和回购，占其收入的近80%。

与此同时，由于之前的整合浪潮失去了动力，用于收购的资金下降了70%。但问题是：该集团的石油产量在2025年创下历史新高，而收入增长了7% ，这意味着减少钻探支出几乎不会损害其底线。

安永的Matt Melnar表示： “今年研究中最明显的信号之一是，石油生产和储备替代正在朝着不同的方向发展。” “储备金替代指标本身不再能说明全部情况。生产商在做出投资决策时，在生产目标、股东回报和长期投资组合弹性之间进行平衡。”

尽管由于钻井效率的提高、技术进步以及向更短周期、高回报资产的战略转变，资本支出下降，但大型石油公司仍成功地增加了产量。从历史上看，更高的产量需要线性增加钻探新井的支出。然而，页岩油公司正在钻探更长的水平井，有时延伸三英里或更长，允许单个表面钻井平台开采更多的含油岩石。同时完成多口井可大幅削减执行时间和服务合同成本。

Additionally, operators are increasingly deploying AI, machine learning and predictive analytics to maximize production efficiency, cut operating costs and extend the lifespan of oil and gas wells. Deep learning models process large 3D and 4D seismic datasets, combining them with historical drilling logs to map out high-permeability zones with higher precision. Predictive analytics evaluate past completion data to determine the volume of proppant required, fluid and pressure needed to fracture a specific sweet spot, ensuring maximum estimated ultimate recovery (EUR). Meanwhile, AI-driven geosteering systems analyze real-time rock properties at the drill bit, automatically adjusting the trajectory to maximize yields. When drilling for natural gas, AI systems are used to continuously adjust gas injection rates through surface and downhole valves thus ensuring the optimal liquid-to-gas ratio is achieved.

The U.S. shale boom broke the old model. For decades, oil production growth meant long, expensive offshore or mega field projects that took years to pay off. Shale flipped that: wells get drilled, fracked, and pumping oil within months. With demand set to plateau and geopolitics this unstable, a 10-year infrastructure bet is a much riskier bet than a short one; operators would rather earn their money back fast than risk getting stuck with assets nobody wants once demand or policy shifts under them.

However, Big Oil cannot continue cutting capex indefinitely. Some oil assets such as Exxon Mobil’s deepwater projects in Guyana require heavy upfront investments but significantly less additional capital to keep going. Others have been leaning heavily on their inventories of Drilled but Uncompleted (DUC) wells to keep production up without spending more on new wells. Back in May, the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) revealed that the total U.S. DUC inventory dipped to approximately 4,972 wells, marking the lowest level since the agency began tracking the metric in 2013. This also marked 14 consecutive months of decline in the DUC count.

面对油价疲软的时期，生产商选择完成以前的钻井，而不是部署新的钻井平台，这在经济上是有意义的，因为完成现有的DUC历史成本约为500万至600万美元，而从头开始钻探和完成新井需要800万至1000万美元。

然而，这需要付出代价：安永报告说，大型石油公司的石油储量因发现和扩建而增加，同比下降11% ，五年来首次未能完全取代产量。这意味着，在全球供应突然紧缩或油价飙升的情况下，美国页岩油生产商在快速增加产量方面的灵活性较低。

“As energy security, industrial competitiveness and AI-related infrastructure investment continue to shape energy markets, US natural gas is increasingly positioned at the center of several of the industry’s most significant demand trends,” EY’s Patrick Jelinek said. “The strength we’re seeing in gas reserves, discoveries and revisions suggests producers are recognizing the opportunity and positioning for a future where natural gas plays an increasingly strategic role in the energy system.”
