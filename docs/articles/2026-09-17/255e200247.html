Tanker Rates Smash $1 Million a Day as Oil Shipping Crisis Deepens

OilPrice · 2026-09-17 07:00 · 机器翻译，仅供参考

The daily rate for commissioning a tanker topped $1 million for the first time in history amid a tightening supply of vessels to carry crude oil and fuels whose owners are willing to dare the Strait of Hormuz. The squeeze is adding extra costs to the price of oil for physical delivery, just as tankers become a cybersecurity target.

Yet the Middle East is not the only place where tankers are commanding much higher rates. Costs are rising at Russia’s key Black Sea port of Novorossiysk because of the risk of Ukrainian drone attacks on both vessels and port infrastructure. In the week to September 6, the key tanker rate for shipping crude oil on an Aframax tanker from Novorossiysk to West India and North China rose for the seventh straight week, by 2.7% and 3.1% from the week prior, respectively, local pricing indexes showed.

Higher freight rates mean higher end prices for the crude, adding to already substantial pain for buyers as the war between the United States and Israel against Iran extends into its seventh month with little prospect of peace on the horizon. This week, prices saw some downward pressure after the U.S. Energy Information Administration estimated an inventory build of over 7 million barrels for last week, but the effect will not last long. Seven million barrels of oil is not really a solution to the world’s deepening supply problem, with Saudi Arabia’s East-West pipeline out, potentially for weeks, after a drone attack. According to the International Energy Agency, global observed oil inventories shed 95 million barrels in August, for a cumulative loss of 507 million barrels since February, equal to around 2.8 million barrels daily. Oil on water also declined by 65 million barrels amid attacks on vessels in the Strait of Hormuz.

It is in this fraught context that U.S. federal authorities said they were investigating a possible cyberattack on tankers traveling from Europe to the United States. The Wall Street Journal reported the news this week, saying at least two vessels had become the target of a cyberattack and were subjected to inspections when they arrived at the Gulf Coast in August.

检查显示，一艘装满原油，另一艘装满液化气的油轮在直布罗陀周围遭到袭击，引发了人们对一个不友好国家可能支持它的怀疑，据《华尔街日报》报道，该报告援引了未透露姓名的政府官员的话。直布罗陀是全球市场的中心能源动脉，日常交通量约为300艘船舶。报告指出，网络攻击可能导致碰撞、泄漏甚至爆炸，因为油轮运行的软件显然可以被黑客入侵。

“For a vessel that’s carrying tens of millions of gallons of crude oil, which is highly volatile, there’s always a risk of fire explosion,” the head of the U.S. Coast Guard’s Cyber Command told the Wall Street Journal. “The atmosphere in the tanks has to be very carefully managed to ensure that you’re not going to get a situation where there’s a fire explosion. And then there’s always a risk of an oil spill,” Rear Admiral Jason Tama explained.

与此同时，由于油轮费率较高，能源市场参与者正在寻求购买自己的船舶，而不是付钱给其他人租一艘，特别是因为保险费用也飙升。“自霍尔木兹海峡关闭以来，二手油轮的价格飙升，一些二手船现在的价值高于新船，因为建造一艘新船需要两到三年的时间，”阿格斯分析师Erica Tsirikou本月早些时候告诉西班牙的ElPaís。

二手油轮也在向新手溢价销售。一艘二手超大型原油运输船现在的售价约为$ 1.82亿，而新造船的售价为$ 1.3亿。“收购这些二手油轮的竞争激烈，这推动了销售价格大幅上涨，”阿格斯的Tsirikou还告诉西班牙日报，并补充说，二手苏伊士型油轮的售价为1.3亿美元，二手阿芙拉型油轮的售价为9500万美元，而新造油轮的售价分别为8900万美元和7500万美元。

These developments were, in fairness, inevitable. The paralysis of the Strait of Hormuz has tipped the tanker market into imbalance, and this imbalance has only deepened since March. And it just got worse, as Saudi Arabia tries to repair its East-West pipeline and cancels oil cargoes that were to be shipped to Europe this month.
