Why The United States’ Belated Critical Minerals Gambit Won’t Stop China

OilPrice · 2026-09-11 07:00 · 机器翻译，仅供参考

Alex Kimani是Safehaven.com的资深金融作家、投资者、工程师和研究员。

U.S. President Donald Trump has undertaken the most aggressive federal intervention in the critical minerals and rare earth sectors ever since he returned to the Oval Office, announcing a flurry of deals as Washington desperately tries to counter China’s dominance in minerals that are powering the energy transition and AI boom. The deals included a hodgepodge of equity and debt packages with dozens of rare earths companies, with MP Materials (NYSE:MP) and U.S. Rare Earths (NASDAQ:USAR) some of the key beneficiaries. Last month, Trump unveiled a $3-billion federal investment in critical minerals projects across the country, part of ongoing efforts to scale domestic production and decouple from Chinese battery supply chains. The highlight of the financing round was a $1.4-billion conditional loan to Sila Nanotechnologies from the Pentagon’s Office of Strategic Capital (OSC), with the California-based startup looking to scale up the production of next-generation battery materials. The United States is now home to a growing number of companies looking to re-invent EV batteries. That’s hardly surprising considering that EVs represent the leading demand driver for critical minerals, accounting for well over half of total global demand for critical minerals like lithium, cobalt and nickel.

Sila Nanotechnologies是一家电池材料公司，专注于硅碳(Si/C)复合阳极的商业化和大规模生产，以取代锂离子电池中的传统石墨。该公司的旗舰产品是Titan Silicon ，这是一种纳米工程硅阳极材料，旨在无缝替代现有的电池制造工艺。与标准石墨电池相比，该公司的电池有望将能量密度提高40% ，同时更轻、更紧凑。该公司于2025年底开始其泰坦硅材料的商业规模生产工艺，摩西湖工厂的目标是生产足够先进的硅碳阳极材料，每年为20,000至50,000辆电动汽车供电。

与此同时，总部位于犹他州的Lilac Solutions正试图通过部署其专利的直接锂提取（ DLE ）技术来从盐水中提取锂，从而撼动采矿业。与传统采矿相比， DLE需要一天而不是两年的时间，通过消除大量的蒸发池，可以回收大约两倍的锂，同时使用99 ％的土地和显着减少的水。

“If you use our technology, you’re producing battery grade lithium carbonate or hydroxide at the site of production,” Raef Sully, CEO of Lilac Solutions, told CNBC. “And you’re bypassing that important step, that processing step that China has a chokehold on today.”

政府的突然推动给观察家留下的印象是，美国的关键矿产供应链即将变得庞大；然而，人们越来越担心，华盛顿对该行业的大量投资仍然不足以赶上中国。

Sino Auto Insights创始人兼董事总经理Tu Le告诉CNBC ： “要实现中国现在拥有的供应链全面规模，需要几十年甚至几百亿美元。” “我们没有几十年的时间。我们有五年、六年、七年的时间来努力提高竞争力。”

“They have built up an incredible lead in terms of technology and manufacturing capabilities across the world,” Richard Wang, CEO of Voya Energy, told CNBC about China’s CATL, the world’s largest EV battery manufacturer. “They are one of the only battery companies in the world that’s not only high in revenue, but is significantly profitable because of how strong their manufacturing and supply chain capabilities are.”

具有讽刺意味的是，特朗普政府的关键政策也可能损害他日以继夜地推动的行业。通过取消拜登时代的气候激励措施，特朗普政府引发了清洁能源投资放缓，冷却了消费者需求，并使可再生能源供应链陷入混乱。也就是说，电动汽车在美国的销量在特朗普政府取消了7500 $的联邦电动汽车税收抵免和燃油经济性标准的回落之后， 2026年第一季度，美国电动汽车销量同比下降27% ，标志着特朗普重返白宫以来连续第二个季度的销量下滑。很明显，一个萎缩的美国电动汽车市场对电池材料公司来说并不是一个好兆头。作者： Alex Kimani ， Oilprice.com
