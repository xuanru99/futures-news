China is on track to import roughly the same volumes of crude oil in September as it did in August, extending the trend of recovering shipments just as crude oil prices soared to above $100 per barrel again.

据日经亚洲报道， Kpler的初步船舶跟踪数据显示， 9月份中国原油进口量约为720万桶/日，与8月份大致相同。

The September-arriving crude cargoes were bought at prices well below $100 per barrel, but the gradual recovery of Chinese purchases from the decade-low in June signal further upward pressure on crude oil prices.

Refiners in China have moved in recent weeks to restock on crude supplies, while prices were still in the $80s per barrel.

8月份，中国原油进口连续第二个月增长，因为炼油厂转向额外的非中东供应，并在出口限制放松的情况下增加了海外燃料出货量。

The August import level was still 23.4% lower compared to the same month last year, but it’s a marked improvement from the June lows of just 7.1 million bpd.

中国在6月份将其原油进口总量降至十年低点，在中东价格高企和供应受限的情况下，进口水平达到了三个月的极低水平。
